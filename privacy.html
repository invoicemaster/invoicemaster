Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 14, 2026

InvoiceMaster is built around a simple promise: your invoice data stays on your device. This page explains exactly what that means and the few small exceptions.

What we don't collect

We do not collect, store, or transmit any of your invoice data. That includes:

  • Your business name, address, tax ID, logo, or other business details
  • Your clients' names, addresses, phone numbers, or any personal information
  • Invoice line items, amounts, dates, notes, or any other invoice content
  • Your preferences, templates, or saved drafts

All of the above lives only in your browser's local storage (IndexedDB and localStorage). It never reaches our servers because we do not run any servers for this product. InvoiceMaster is a static website — there is no backend that could receive your data even if we wanted it to.

What we do collect

We use Google Analytics 4 to understand which pages get traffic, where visitors come from, and which industries and templates people care about. This helps us decide what to build next. The data Google Analytics collects is anonymous and aggregate. Specifically:

  • Page views (which URLs were visited)
  • Referrer (the website you came from, if any)
  • Approximate location (country and city, derived from IP — your full IP is not stored)
  • Device type, browser, and operating system
  • Anonymous engagement signals (time on page, scroll depth, outbound link clicks)

None of this is tied to anything you type into an invoice. Google Analytics does not see your invoice content.

Cookies

Google Analytics sets one cookie (_ga) to distinguish unique visitors. It contains a randomly generated identifier — not your name, email, or any data you've entered. If you'd rather opt out, install the official Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on, or block third-party cookies for our domain.

We do not use any other tracking cookies, advertising pixels, social-media beacons, or third-party scripts beyond Google Analytics itself.

Where your data lives

When you fill in an invoice, business profile, or client record, that data is written to your browser's local IndexedDB store. It stays on your device. If you:

  • Clear your browser data, all your invoices are deleted forever (we cannot recover them)
  • Use InvoiceMaster on a different device or browser, your data is not synced — each device has its own local copy
  • Uninstall the PWA, the underlying browser data may persist; clear it from your browser settings if you want it gone

Third parties

The only third party that receives any data from InvoiceMaster is Google (for Google Analytics). We do not share, sell, rent, or trade any information to anyone else. There are no ad networks, no marketing pixels, no data brokers.

Children

InvoiceMaster is not directed at children under 13. Because we do not collect personal data, there is no specific information about children to disclose.

Your rights

Because we do not store any of your business or client data on our servers, there is nothing for us to delete on your request. To delete the data on your device, clear the site's data from your browser settings, or use the Export feature to back it up first.

For the anonymous Google Analytics data, you can opt out using the add-on above. Under GDPR and CCPA, you have the right to request a copy of any personal data held about you — please email us if you have such a request.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll bump the "Last updated" date at the top. Material changes will be flagged in the site footer for at least 30 days.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Email us and we'll respond within a few days.

Found a bug or missing a feature? Let us know — we ship fixes in a few days.
