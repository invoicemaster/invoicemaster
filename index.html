FAQ

Is it really free?

Yes — no signup, no subscription, no watermarks.

Where is my data stored?

Locally in your browser (IndexedDB). Nothing is uploaded.

Can I save invoices as PDF?

Yes. Click Print and choose Save as PDF.

Will my saved invoices follow me to another device?

No — data is local to the browser you used. Export the PDF if you need to share or archive.

Which template should I pick?

Match it to your business: Freelancer for services, Retail for product sales, Contractor for trades, Auto Shop for mechanics and body shops, Hospitality for restaurants. Classic, Modern, and Band Blue work for any business.
